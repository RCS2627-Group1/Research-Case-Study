
For our initial experiments, lets work with the same two datasets so that we can compare our results more easily.

## 1. Reddit Sample – `dog_whistle_reddit_sample_5000.csv`

This is a sample of 5,000 posts from the **Informal Potential Dog Whistles from Reddit** dataset by SALT-NLP.

- Contains Reddit posts that can be used to explore potential dog whistles and their usage in different contexts.
- This will be our main dataset for the experiments.

## 2. Silent Signals Dataset - 'silent_signals_reference_dictionary.csv'

This dataset contains known dog-whistle expressions and examples of their usage.

- We will mainly use it as a reference for identifying and matching known expressions.
- It can be useful for dictionary-based matching, co-occurrence analysis and comparing our discovered candidates with known expressions.

## 3. Saving Outputs

Please save your experiment results as **CSV files** and upload them to the shared project repository with clear file names so we know which experiment the results belong to.

Depending on your experiment, your CSV could include:

- Candidate words or expressions
- Frequency counts or scores (PMI, LLR, etc.)
- Ranking of the candidate expressions
- Example posts or contexts where they appear
- Any labels or predictions generated by your method

**The idea for now is to keep things simple, test the individual approaches and then compare what works best before deciding on the final pipeline.**
